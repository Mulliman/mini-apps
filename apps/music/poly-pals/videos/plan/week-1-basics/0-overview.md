# Week 1 Plan: PolyRhythm Basics

## Theme Overview
Week 1 introduces the fundamental polyrhythmic collisions (3 vs 4, 2 vs 3, 4 vs 5, 3 vs 5, 5 vs 6) using clean, accessible major and pentatonic voicings.

## Schedule & Content Outline
- **Day 1**: `1-3-vs-4.md` — The Classic 3 against 4 Polyrhythm (C Major 7)
- **Day 2**: `2-2-vs-3.md` — The Gentle 2 against 3 Hemiola (G Major triad)
- **Day 3**: `3-4-vs-5.md` — The Modern 4 against 5 Pulse (A Minor 7)
- **Day 4**: `4-3-vs-5.md` — The Hypnotic 3 against 5 Interlock (F Major 7)
- **Day 5**: `5-5-vs-6.md` — The Dense 5 against 6 Shimmer (E Minor 7)
- **Day 6**: `6-binary-stack.md` — Pure Binary Acceleration: 2, 4, 8, 16 (C Major)
- **Day 7**: `7-grand-polyrhythm.md` — Grand Polyrhythmic Cascade: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 (C Major 9)
