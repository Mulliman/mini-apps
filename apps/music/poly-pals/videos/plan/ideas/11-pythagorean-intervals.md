# Idea 11: Pythagorean Frequency Ratios

- **Theme Slug**: `pythagorean-intervals`
- **Category**: Music Theory / Acoustic History
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Exploring ancient Pythagorean tuning ratios where frequency intervals (Octave $1:2$, Fifth $2:3$, Fourth $3:4$, Major Third $4:5$, Minor Third $5:6$) are converted into exact rhythmic speed signatures. Demonstrating how interval harmony directly corresponds to rhythmic resonance.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Major Triad & Dyads (`C3, G3, C4, E4, G4, A4`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: Exact integer ratios: `2:3` (Fifth), `3:4` (Fourth), `4:5` (Major 3rd), `5:6` (Minor 3rd), `8:9` (Major 2nd).
- **Voicing Strategy**: Pair the lower note of the interval with the lower integer signature, and the higher pitch note with the higher integer signature.
