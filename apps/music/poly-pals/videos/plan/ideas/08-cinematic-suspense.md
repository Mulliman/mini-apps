# Idea 08: Cinematic & Dark Ambient Tension

- **Theme Slug**: `cinematic-suspense`
- **Category**: Film Score / Dark Ambient
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Building cinematic tension and suspense using minor 7th chord clusters, natural minor modalities, and accelerating polyrhythmic pulses. As higher speed lanes enter, the rhythmic density creates intense kinetic energy akin to Hans Zimmer or Christopher Nolan film cues.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: A Minor 9 (`A3, C4, E4, G4, B4`), E Minor 11 (`E3, G4, B4, D5, A5`), D Minor 7 (`D3, F4, A4, C5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: `3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Deep booming sub-bass roots (`A3, E3`) on slow signatures; tension-building cluster notes (`G4, A4, B4`) on high-frequency signatures.
