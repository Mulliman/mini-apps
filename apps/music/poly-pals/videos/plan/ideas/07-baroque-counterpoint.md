# Idea 07: Baroque Inventions & Counterpoint

- **Theme Slug**: `baroque-counterpoint`
- **Category**: Historical / Classical Counterpoint
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Inspired by J.S. Bach's Two-Part and Three-Part Inventions. We voice polyphonic, contrapuntal melodic voices that interact across complementary polyrhythmic ratios ($2:3, 3:4, 4:6$). Rhythmic friction is combined with classical voice leading.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Major (`C3, E4, G4`), A Minor (`A3, C4, E4`), D Minor (`D3, F4, A4`), G Major (`G3, B4, D5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: `2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Bass cantus firmus on signature 2 or 3 (`C3, A3`); middle voice counter-subject on signature 4 or 6 (`E4, C4`); top ornamentation on signature 8 or 12 (`G5, B5`).
