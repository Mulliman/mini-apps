# Idea 01: Fibonacci Ratios & Golden Spirals

- **Theme Slug**: `fibonacci-grooves`
- **Category**: Mathematical / Organic Growth
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Polyrhythms built upon ratios from the Fibonacci sequence ($1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13$). As the week progresses, rhythmic subdivisions spiral outwards from simple 2:3 ratio collisions to complex 5:8:13 multi-lane spirals. Harmonic voicings use expanding intervallic structures derived from pentatonic and major 9th chords.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Major 7 (`C3, E4, G4, B4`), A Minor 9 (`A3, C4, E4, G4, B5`), F Major 9 (`F3, A4, C5, E5, G5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: Fibonacci signatures: `2, 3, 5, 8, 13`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Assign lowest Fibonacci signatures (2, 3) to bass roots (`C3, A3`) and higher signatures (5, 8, 13) to upper extension shimmer (`G4, B4, E5`).
