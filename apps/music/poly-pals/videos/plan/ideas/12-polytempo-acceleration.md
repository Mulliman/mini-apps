# Idea 12: Polytempo Acceleration Stacks

- **Theme Slug**: `polytempo-acceleration`
- **Category**: Experimental / Tempo Ratios
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Exploring multi-tempo stacks where layers operate at relative tempo multipliers ($1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2.0x$). Using signature ratios like $4:5:6:7:8$, each lane acts like a clock running at a distinct tempo, producing accelerating cascade effects as lanes enter.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Major 9 (`C3, E4, G4, B4, D5`), A Minor 11 (`A3, C4, E4, G4, D5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: `4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Slow 1.0x tempo on bass anchor; intermediate tempos on tenor/alto voicings; hyper-tempo 2.0x on top soprano shimmer.
