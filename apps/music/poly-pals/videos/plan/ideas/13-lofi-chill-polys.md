# Idea 13: Lofi Hip-Hop Poly-Vibes

- **Theme Slug**: `lofi-chill-polys`
- **Category**: Chill / Lofi Hip-Hop
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Ultra-relaxing, ambient lofi hip-hop grooves using warm C Major 9, A Minor 9, and F Major 7 pentatonic voicings. Relaxed polyrhythms ($3:4, 3:5, 4:7$) drift gently across slow bar durations (approx 3.0s/bar), creating cozy background study beats.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Major 9 (`C3, E4, G4, B4, D5`), A Minor 9 (`A3, C4, E4, G4, B4`), F Major 7 (`F3, A4, C5, E5`), D Minor 9 (`D3, F4, A4, C5, E5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: `3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Muted, gentle pitch distribution. Deep warm bass (`C3, F3`), soft inner chord harmony (`E4, A4`), and sparse high pentatonic drops (`D5, E5`).
