# Idea 03: Extended Jazz Harmonies

- **Theme Slug**: `extended-jazz`
- **Category**: Genre / Harmonic Sophistication
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Pairing lush, extended jazz chord structures (Major 9th, Minor 11th, Dominant 9th) with smooth swing-adjacent polyrhythmic signatures ($3, 4, 5, 7$). This theme focuses on how polyrhythmic alignment enhances harmonic voice leading.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Major 9 (`C3, E4, G4, B4, D5`), A Minor 11 (`A3, C4, E4, G4, D5`), D Minor 9 (`D3, F4, A4, C5, E5`), G Dominant 9 (`G3, B4, D5, F5, A5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: `3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 12`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Bass notes on slow 3s and 4s; rich 7th, 9th, and 11th extensions on fast 5s, 7s, and 9s.
