## 1. Musical Concept

- **Fill Ratio**: **3:4** (3 against 4)
- **Concept**: The classic half-note triplet fill (3 over 4). In 4/4 time, spacing 3 evenly-spaced pulses across the bar creates the quintessential cross-rhythm tension used by drummers and rhythm sections across rock, pop, jazz, and gospel.
- **Backing Foundation (4/4 Triad)**:
  - `1`: Downbeat master pulse (Whole notes) — Root
  - `2`: Half-note pulse (Beats 1 & 3) — Third
  - `4`: Quarter-note pulse (Beats 1, 2, 3, 4) — Fifth
- **Fill Lane**: Signature `3` (Triplets). Muted for bars 1–3 of each cycle, taking over the quarter notes on bar 4 on the exact same pitch.
- **Harmonic Cycles & Accelerando**:
  - **Cycle 1 (Bars 1–4, 96 BPM / 2.50s)**: **C Major** (`C3 - E3 - G3`), Fill plays `G3` (196.00 Hz)
  - **Cycle 2 (Bars 5–8, 109 BPM / 2.20s)**: **A Minor** (`A3 - C4 - E4`), Fill plays `E4` (329.63 Hz)
  - **Cycle 3 (Bars 9–12, 126 BPM / 1.90s)**: **F Major** (`F3 - A3 - C4`), Fill plays `C4` (261.63 Hz)
  - **Cycle 4 (Bars 13–16, 145 BPM / 1.65s)**: **G Major** (`G3 - B3 - D4`), Fill plays `D4` (293.66 Hz)
  - **Cycle 5 (Bars 17–20, 171 BPM / 1.40s)**: **C Major Climax** (`C3 - E3 - G3`), Fill plays `G3` (196.00 Hz)
  - **Bars 21–23**: Final resolution on C Major and ring-out
