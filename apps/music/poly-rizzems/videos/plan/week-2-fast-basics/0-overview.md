# Week 2 Plan: Fast Polyrhythm Basics (Uptempo)

## Theme Overview
Week 2 revisits the 7 fundamental 2-rhythm polyrhythmic collisions (3 vs 4, 2 vs 3, 4 vs 5, 3 vs 5, 3 vs 7, 4 vs 7, 5 vs 7) at a noticeably faster, high-energy tempo (`barDuration: 1.8s` vs Week 1's `2.6s`). The faster pace tests rhythmic agility, rapid ear tracking, and perceptual blending into pitch-like grooves.
