## 1. Musical Concept

- **Fill Ratio**: **6:3** (6 against 3, or 2 notes per beat)
- **Concept**: The classic eighth-note waltz double-time fill. Spacing 6 notes across 3 quarter-note beats creates an energetic driving subdivision (2:1 per beat) before slamming back into the quarter-note groove.
- **Backing Foundation (3/4 Groove)**:
  - `1`: Downbeat master bass pulse (dotted half note) — Root (`F3`, 174.61 Hz)
  - `3`: Steady waltz quarter-note pulse (beats 1, 2, 3) — Fifth (`C4`, 261.63 Hz)
- **Fill Lane**: Signature `6` (Sextuplet). Muted for bars 0–2 of each 4-bar phrase. On the 4th bar (bar index 3, 7, 11, 15, 19), Lane `3` mutes while the Fill Lane un-mutes on the **exact same pitch** (`C4`), doubling the pulse cleanly into 6 hits!
- **Harmonic Cycles & Accelerando**:
  - **Cycle 1 (Bars 0–3, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **F Major** (`F3 – C4`), Fill plays `C4`
  - **Cycle 2 (Bars 4–7, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **D Minor** (`D3 – A3`), Fill plays `A3`
  - **Cycle 3 (Bars 8–11, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **Bb Major** (`Bb3 – F4`), Fill plays `F4`
  - **Cycle 4 (Bars 12–15, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **C Major** (`C3 – G3`), Fill plays `G3`
  - **Cycle 5 (Bars 16–19, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **F Major Climax** (`F3 – C4`), Fill plays `C4` (accelerating to 160 BPM / 1.125s on bar 18 for the final two bars)
  - **Outro (Bars 20–22)**: Immediate mute of fill lane on downbeat of 20, clean 3-bar unwind resolving on F Major.
