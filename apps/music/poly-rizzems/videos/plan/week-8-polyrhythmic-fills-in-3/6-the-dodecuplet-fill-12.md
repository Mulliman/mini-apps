## 1. Musical Concept

- **Fill Ratio**: **12:3** (12 against 3, or exactly 4 notes per beat)
- **Concept**: Quadruple-speed sixteenth-note waltz barrage. 12 strikes evenly divided across 3 beats delivers a razor-sharp, blistering roll (4 notes per beat) that charges the turnaround with unrelenting momentum.
- **Backing Foundation (3/4 Groove)**:
  - `1`: Downbeat master bass pulse (dotted half note) — Root (`E3`, 164.81 Hz)
  - `3`: Steady waltz quarter-note pulse (beats 1, 2, 3) — Fifth (`B3`, 246.94 Hz)
- **Fill Lane**: Signature `12` (Dodecuplet). Muted for bars 0–2 of each 4-bar phrase. On the 4th bar (bar index 3, 7, 11, 15, 19), Lane `3` mutes while the Fill Lane un-mutes on the **exact same pitch** (`B3`), firing a 12-note sixteenth-note cascade across the 3 beats!
- **Harmonic Cycles & Accelerando**:
  - **Cycle 1 (Bars 0–3, 95 BPM / 1.895s)**: **E Minor** (`E3 – B3`), Fill plays `B3`
  - **Cycle 2 (Bars 4–7, 95 BPM / 1.895s)**: **C Major** (`C3 – G3`), Fill plays `G3`
  - **Cycle 3 (Bars 8–11, 95 BPM / 1.895s)**: **A Minor** (`A3 – E4`), Fill plays `E4`
  - **Cycle 4 (Bars 12–15, 115 BPM / 1.565s)**: **B7** (`B3 – F#4`), Fill plays `F#4` (369.99 Hz)
  - **Cycle 5 (Bars 16–19, 115 BPM / 1.565s)**: **E Minor Climax** (`E3 – B3`), Fill plays `B3` (accelerating to 115 BPM / 1.565s on bar 18 for the final two bars)
  - **Outro (Bars 20–22)**: Immediate mute of fill lane on downbeat of 20, clean 3-bar unwind resolving on E Minor.
