## 1. Musical Concept

- **Fill Ratio**: **5:3** (5 against 3)
- **Concept**: Quintuplets in 3/4. Spacing 5 pulses evenly across 3 quarter notes creates an exotic, rolling polyrhythmic tension popular in modern jazz, gospel, and prog-metal waltzes.
- **Backing Foundation (3/4 Groove)**:
  - `1`: Downbeat master bass pulse (dotted half note) — Root (`A3`, 220.00 Hz)
  - `3`: Steady waltz quarter-note pulse (beats 1, 2, 3) — Fifth (`E4`, 329.63 Hz)
- **Fill Lane**: Signature `5` (Quintuplet). Muted for bars 0–2 of each 4-bar phrase. On the 4th bar (bar index 3, 7, 11, 15, 19), Lane `3` mutes while the Fill Lane un-mutes on the **exact same pitch** (`E4`), ripping 5 equidistant hits across the 3 beats!
- **Harmonic Cycles & Accelerando**:
  - **Cycle 1 (Bars 0–3, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **A Minor** (`A3 – E4`), Fill plays `E4`
  - **Cycle 2 (Bars 4–7, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **F Major** (`F3 – C4`), Fill plays `C4`
  - **Cycle 3 (Bars 8–11, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **D Minor** (`D3 – A3`), Fill plays `A3`
  - **Cycle 4 (Bars 12–15, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **E7** (`E3 – B3`), Fill plays `B3`
  - **Cycle 5 (Bars 16–19, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **A Minor Climax** (`A3 – E4`), Fill plays `E4` (accelerating to 160 BPM / 1.125s on bar 18 for the final two bars)
  - **Outro (Bars 20–22)**: Immediate mute of fill lane on downbeat of 20, clean 3-bar unwind resolving on A Minor.
