## 1. Musical Concept

- **Fill Ratio**: **8:3** (8 against 3)
- **Concept**: Binary octuplet burst in 3/4. 8 evenly spaced strikes colliding against 3 quarter notes ($2\frac{2}{3}$ notes per beat) creates an intense, driving polyrhythmic cross-current reminiscent of math-rock and contemporary drum cadences.
- **Backing Foundation (3/4 Groove)**:
  - `1`: Downbeat master bass pulse (dotted half note) — Root (`D3`, 146.83 Hz)
  - `3`: Steady waltz quarter-note pulse (beats 1, 2, 3) — Fifth (`A3`, 220.00 Hz)
- **Fill Lane**: Signature `8` (Octuplet). Muted for bars 0–2 of each 4-bar phrase. On the 4th bar (bar index 3, 7, 11, 15, 19), Lane `3` mutes while the Fill Lane un-mutes on the **exact same pitch** (`A3`), releasing 8 equidistant hits across the 3-beat measure!
- **Harmonic Cycles & Accelerando**:
  - **Cycle 1 (Bars 0–3, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **D Minor** (`D3 – A3`), Fill plays `A3`
  - **Cycle 2 (Bars 4–7, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **Bb Major** (`Bb3 – F4`), Fill plays `F4`
  - **Cycle 3 (Bars 8–11, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **G Minor** (`G3 – D4`), Fill plays `D4`
  - **Cycle 4 (Bars 12–15, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **A7** (`A3 – E4`), Fill plays `E4`
  - **Cycle 5 (Bars 16–19, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **D Minor Climax** (`D3 – A3`), Fill plays `A3` (accelerating to 160 BPM / 1.125s on bar 18 for the final two bars)
  - **Outro (Bars 20–22)**: Immediate mute of fill lane on downbeat of 20, clean 3-bar unwind resolving on D Minor.
