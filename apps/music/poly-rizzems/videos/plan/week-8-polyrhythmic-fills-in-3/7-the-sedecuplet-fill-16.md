## 1. Musical Concept

- **Fill Ratio**: **16:3** (16 against 3)
- **Concept**: The grand master hyper-speed sedecuplet finale. 16 equidistant pulses firing across 3 quarter notes (over 5 notes per beat) produces an astonishing, hypnotic laser-beam flurry before the band lands triumphantly back on the downbeat.
- **Backing Foundation (3/4 Groove)**:
  - `1`: Downbeat master bass pulse (dotted half note) — Root (`Bb3`, 233.08 Hz)
  - `3`: Steady waltz quarter-note pulse (beats 1, 2, 3) — Fifth (`F4`, 349.23 Hz)
- **Fill Lane**: Signature `16` (Sedecuplet). Muted for bars 0–2 of each 4-bar phrase. On the 4th bar (bar index 3, 7, 11, 15, 19), Lane `3` mutes while the Fill Lane un-mutes on the **exact same pitch** (`F4`), unleashing a breathtaking 16-note hyper-speed stream across the 3 beats!
- **Harmonic Cycles & Accelerando**:
  - **Cycle 1 (Bars 0–3, 85 BPM / 2.118s)**: **Bb Major** (`Bb3 – F4`), Fill plays `F4`
  - **Cycle 2 (Bars 4–7, 85 BPM / 2.118s)**: **G Minor** (`G3 – D4`), Fill plays `D4`
  - **Cycle 3 (Bars 8–11, 85 BPM / 2.118s)**: **Eb Major** (`Eb3 – Bb3`), Fill plays `Bb3`
  - **Cycle 4 (Bars 12–15, 105 BPM / 1.714s)**: **F Major** (`F3 – C4`), Fill plays `C4`
  - **Cycle 5 (Bars 16–19, 105 BPM / 1.714s)**: **Bb Major Climax** (`Bb3 – F4`), Fill plays `F4` (accelerating to 105 BPM / 1.714s on bar 18 for the final two bars)
  - **Outro (Bars 20–22)**: Immediate mute of fill lane on downbeat of 20, clean 3-bar unwind resolving on Bb Major.
