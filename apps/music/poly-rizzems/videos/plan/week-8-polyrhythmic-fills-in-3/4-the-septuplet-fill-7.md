## 1. Musical Concept

- **Fill Ratio**: **7:3** (7 against 3)
- **Concept**: Prime septuplet fluidity in 3/4. Dividing 3 quarter notes into 7 equal beats yields an asymmetric, liquid polyrhythm that floats unpredictably across the triple meter before snapping back into focus.
- **Backing Foundation (3/4 Groove)**:
  - `1`: Downbeat master bass pulse (dotted half note) — Root (`G3`, 196.00 Hz)
  - `3`: Steady waltz quarter-note pulse (beats 1, 2, 3) — Fifth (`D4`, 293.66 Hz)
- **Fill Lane**: Signature `7` (Septuplet). Muted for bars 0–2 of each 4-bar phrase. On the 4th bar (bar index 3, 7, 11, 15, 19), Lane `3` mutes while the Fill Lane un-mutes on the **exact same pitch** (`D4`), releasing 7 equidistant strikes across the 3 beats!
- **Harmonic Cycles & Accelerando**:
  - **Cycle 1 (Bars 0–3, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **G Major** (`G3 – D4`), Fill plays `D4`
  - **Cycle 2 (Bars 4–7, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **E Minor** (`E3 – B3`), Fill plays `B3`
  - **Cycle 3 (Bars 8–11, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **C Major** (`C3 – G3`), Fill plays `G3`
  - **Cycle 4 (Bars 12–15, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **D Major** (`D3 – A3`), Fill plays `A3`
  - **Cycle 5 (Bars 16–19, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **G Major Climax** (`G3 – D4`), Fill plays `D4` (accelerating to 160 BPM / 1.125s on bar 18 for the final two bars)
  - **Outro (Bars 20–22)**: Immediate mute of fill lane on downbeat of 20, clean 3-bar unwind resolving on G Major.
