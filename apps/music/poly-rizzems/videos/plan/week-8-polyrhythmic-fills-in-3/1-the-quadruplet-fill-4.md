## 1. Musical Concept

- **Fill Ratio**: **4:3** (4 against 3)
- **Concept**: The classic 4-over-3 cross-rhythm hemiola. In 3/4 waltz time, dividing the 3-beat measure into 4 equidistant strikes creates that signature rolling syncopation before locking back onto beat 1.
- **Backing Foundation (3/4 Groove)**:
  - `1`: Downbeat master bass pulse (dotted half note) — Root (`C3`, 130.81 Hz)
  - `3`: Steady waltz quarter-note pulse (beats 1, 2, 3) — Fifth (`G3`, 196.00 Hz)
- **Fill Lane**: Signature `4` (Quadruplet). Muted for bars 0–2 of each 4-bar phrase. On the 4th bar (bar index 3, 7, 11, 15, 19), Lane `3` mutes while the Fill Lane un-mutes on the **exact same pitch** (`G3`), replacing the 3 quarter notes with 4 equidistant hits!
- **Harmonic Cycles & Accelerando**:
  - **Cycle 1 (Bars 0–3, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **C Major** (`C3 – G3`), Fill plays `G3`
  - **Cycle 2 (Bars 4–7, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **A Minor** (`A3 – E4`), Fill plays `E4`
  - **Cycle 3 (Bars 8–11, 135 BPM / 1.333s)**: **F Major** (`F3 – C4`), Fill plays `C4`
  - **Cycle 4 (Bars 12–15, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **G Major** (`G3 – D4`), Fill plays `D4`
  - **Cycle 5 (Bars 16–19, 160 BPM / 1.125s)**: **C Major Climax** (`C3 – G3`), Fill plays `G3` (accelerating to 160 BPM / 1.125s on bar 18 for the final two bars)
  - **Outro (Bars 20–22)**: Immediate mute of fill lane on downbeat of 20, clean 3-bar unwind resolving on C Major.
