# Week 6 Plan: Famous Chord Progressions as Polyrhythms

## Theme Overview
Week 6 sonifies the most iconic chord progressions in music history by mapping their Roman numeral scale degrees directly into polyrhythmic signatures. Instead of stacking a massive static wall of lanes, Week 6 introduces a lean **mutating-lane architecture**: 4 to 5 persistent tracks on screen (1 tempo pulse + 3–4 polyrhythm lanes) that mutate their pitches and colours dynamically on bar boundaries to perform authentic chord progressions in their original recorded keys.

Every day is anchored directly to a world-famous anthem—spanning arena pop, neo-soul jazz, southern classic rock, 90s alternative anthems, foundational Latin rock 'n' roll, Ray Charles' Spanish minor descent, and 1950s doo-wop.

  - **Song Anchor**: Lynyrd Skynyrd — *"Sweet Home Alabama"*
  - **Key & Chords**: D Mixolydian (`D → C → G`)
  - **Polyrhythms**: `1, 7, 4`

  - **Song Anchor**: The Cranberries — *"Zombie"*

  - **Polyrhythms**: `1, 6, 3, 7`
