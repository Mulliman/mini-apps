# Week 4 Plan: Major Chord Degrees in Polyrhythm

## Theme Overview
Week 4 maps the harmonic scale degrees of the 7 most popular and widely used **Major Chord variations** directly into active polyrhythmic time signatures. All 7 tracks are anchored in the key of **C Major** (`C3` root) using only natural notes, allowing viewers to see and hear how the addition of suspensions, 7ths, 9ths, 6ths, 11ths, and 13ths transforms both harmonic color and rhythmic complexity.

## Schedule & Content Outline
- **Day 1**: `1-c-major-triad-1-3-5.md` — C Major Triad: The Fundamental Harmonic Pulse (`1, 3, 5` in C Major)
- **Day 2**: `2-c-sus4-1-4-5.md` — C Sus4: The Floating Suspension (`1, 4, 5` with 4th replacing 3rd)
- **Day 3**: `3-c-major-7-1-3-5-7.md` — C Major 7: Neo-Soul & Lofi Warmth (`1, 3, 5, 7` introducing prime 7)
- **Day 4**: `4-c-add9-1-3-5-9.md` — C Add9: The Acoustic "Mu Major" Leap (`1, 3, 5, 9` leaping to 9th)
- **Day 5**: `5-c-6-9-1-3-5-6-9.md` — C 6/9: The Pentatonic Chord (`1, 3, 5, 6, 9` pentatonic scale as a chord)
- **Day 6**: `6-c-major-9-1-3-5-7-9.md` — C Major 9: Quintessential Jazz Harmony (`1, 3, 5, 7, 9` odd degree stack)
- **Day 7**: `7-c-major-13-1-3-5-7-9-11-13.md` — C Major 13: The Complete Diatonic Spectrum (`1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13` all 7 degrees)
