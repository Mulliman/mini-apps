# Idea 04: Minimalism & Phase Shifting

- **Theme Slug**: `minimalist-phase`
- **Category**: Experimental / Modern Classical
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Inspired by Steve Reich and Terry Riley. Identical or closely related rhythmic patterns and pitch structures that are slightly offset or run at slightly different subdivisions, causing voices to gradually drift in and out of phase.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Pentatonic (`C3, D4, E4, G4, A4`), A Minor Pentatonic (`A3, C4, D4, E4, G4`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: Micro-offset signatures and contrasting subdivisions (`4, 5, 8, 10, 12, 16`).
- **Voicing Strategy**: Repetitive single-note or dyad lines across staggered entry points, highlighting phase phenomena as textures combine.
