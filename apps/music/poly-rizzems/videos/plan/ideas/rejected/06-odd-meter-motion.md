# Idea 06: Odd Meters in Motion

- **Theme Slug**: `odd-meter-motion`
- **Category**: Progressive / Asymmetric Rhythms
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Exploring asymmetrical and odd-numbered metric pulses ($5/8, 7/8, 9/8, 11/8, 13/8$) overlaid against standard binary quarter notes. This creates a pushing and pulling feeling of metric modulation and asymmetry.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: A Minor 7 (`A3, C4, E4, G4`), F Major 7 (`F3, A4, C5, E5`), G Dominant 7 (`G3, B4, D5, F5`), D Minor 9 (`D3, F4, A4, C5, E5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: Odd meter signatures `5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17` against binary `4, 8`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Steady binary pulse anchors the bass while odd-meter signatures dance on high arpeggios.
