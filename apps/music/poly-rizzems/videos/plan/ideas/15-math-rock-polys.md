# Idea 15: Math Rock Complex Stacks

- **Theme Slug**: `math-rock-polys`
- **Category**: Rock / Math Rock Tapping
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
High-energy, arpeggiated finger-tapping guitar aesthetics translated into fast, interlocking polyrhythmic signatures ($7:4, 11:8, 5:8, 9:4$). Bright, sparkling C Major 7, F Major 7, and A Minor 9 voicings create energetic, angular math-rock grooves.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Major 7 (`C3, E4, G4, B4, D5`), F Major 7 (`F3, A4, C5, E5`), A Minor 9 (`A3, C4, E4, G4, B5`), D Minor 11 (`D3, F4, A4, C5, G5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: `5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 14, 16`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Fast energetic signatures assigned to crisp arpeggiated natural notes (`B4, C5, E5, G5`) to mimic high-register two-handed guitar tapping.
