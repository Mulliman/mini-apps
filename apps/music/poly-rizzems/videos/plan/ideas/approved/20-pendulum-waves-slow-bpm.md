# Idea 20: The Pendulum Waves (Slow BPM & High Beat Meters)

## Status: Approved

## Overview
Ultra-slow tempo (20–25 BPM) combined with high beat meters (signatures 11 through 19, or 8–10 lane arrangements) to produce acoustic micro-phasing, pendulum wave visual dynamics, and intensely satisfying unison downbeat resolutions.

At standard tempos (120+ BPM), high meters like 17 or 19 turn into buzzing drones (35+ notes/sec). But at **24 BPM** (1 bar = 2.50s), each pulse sounds between 4.4 and 7.6 notes/sec — creating articulate, melodic, chime-like cascades. Adjacent meters drift apart into shifting serpentine interference patterns mid-bar, then hit a thunderous, super-satisfying unison downbeat every 2.5 seconds.
