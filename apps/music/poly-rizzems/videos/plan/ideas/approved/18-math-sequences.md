# Idea 18: Famous Math Sequences in Polyrhythm

- **Theme Slug**: `math-sequences`
- **Category**: Mathematical / Theoretical Acoustics
- **Status**: Approved

## Core Concept
Translating humanity's most iconic and celebrated mathematical sequences into hypnotic polyrhythmic structures. Each day maps an entire famous mathematical sequence directly into active polyrhythmic signatures ($1$ to $16$), exploring how recurring formulas, recursive geometry, and prime arithmetic translate into acoustic patterns. 

Harmonic voicings for each sequence are engineered using natural notes (C3–C6) to mirror each sequence's mathematical soul—from clean binary doubling in Powers of 2 to the golden intervals of Fibonacci and the uncompromising, non-repeating collisions of pure Primes.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords & Voicings**:
  - **Powers of 2** ($1, 2, 4, 8, 16$): C Major 7 (`C3, G3, E4, B4, C5`) — Crystalline octave doublings and metric symmetry.
  - **Square Numbers** ($1, 4, 9, 16$): D Minor 7 (`D3, A3, F4, C5`) — Geometric power quad clash ($9:16$ against $1:4$).
  - **Triangular Numbers** ($1, 3, 6, 10, 15$): F Major 9 (`F3, C4, A4, E5, G5`) — Arithmetic step acceleration ($+2, +3, +4, +5$).
  - **Fibonacci Sequence** ($1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13$): A Minor 9 (`A3, E4, G4, C5, E5, B5`) — The Golden Spiral converging towards $\phi \approx 1.618$.
  - **Tribonacci Sequence** ($1, 2, 4, 7, 13$): G Dominant 9 (`G3, D4, F4, B4, A5`) — 3-step recursive memory with the angular crunch of 7 and 13.
  - **Catalan Numbers** ($1, 2, 5, 14$): E Minor 11 (`E3, B3, G4, D5`) — Deep bass foundation exploding into high-speed 14-beat combinatorial flurry.
  - **Prime Numbers** ($2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13$): C Major 13 (`C3, G3, E4, B4, D5, A5`) — Grand Finale apex; zero shared factors creating continuous hypnotic collisions.
- **Rhythmic Families**: Binary ($1, 2, 4, 8, 16$), Ternary ($3, 6, 9, 15$), Quinary ($5, 10$), Septenary ($7, 14$), and Prime Undecenary/Tridecenary ($11, 13$).
- **Voicing Strategy**: Ascending speed mapping: slow signatures anchor low modal fundamentals (C3–F3); fast signatures trigger high sparkling extensions (C5–A5).
