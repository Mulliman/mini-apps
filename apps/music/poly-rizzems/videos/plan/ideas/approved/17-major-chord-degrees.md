# Idea 17: Major Chord Degrees in Polyrhythm

- **Theme Slug**: `major-chord-degrees`
- **Category**: Music Theory / Harmonic Evolution
- **Status**: Approved

## Core Concept
Mapping the harmonic scale degrees of the 7 most popular and essential Major chord variations directly into polyrhythmic time signatures. Starting with the fundamental Major Triad ($1, 3, 5$), the week progressively introduces suspensions, 7ths, add9s, 6/9 pentatonic harmonies, 9ths, and finishes on the complete 7-note Major 13th chord ($1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13$).

## Musical Blueprint
- **Key**: All chords rooted in C Major (`C3` base) using natural notes (`C3–A5`).
- **Degree Formulas & Signatures**:
  - Day 1: Major Triad (`1, 3, 5`) — `C3, E4, G4`
  - Day 2: Suspended 4th / Sus4 (`1, 4, 5`) — `C3, F4, G4`
  - Day 3: Major 7th / Maj7 (`1, 3, 5, 7`) — `C3, E4, G4, B4`
  - Day 4: Add9 / "Mu Major" (`1, 3, 5, 9`) — `C3, E4, G4, D5`
  - Day 5: 6/9 Pentatonic Chord (`1, 3, 5, 6, 9`) — `C3, E4, G4, A4, D5`
  - Day 6: Major 9th / Maj9 (`1, 3, 5, 7, 9`) — `C3, E4, G4, B4, D5`
  - Day 7: Major 13th (`1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13`) — `C3, E4, G4, B4, D5, F5, A5`
- **Voicing Strategy**: Bass root `C3` on slow downbeat pulse `1`, ascending chord degrees mapped strictly to ascending time signature speeds.
