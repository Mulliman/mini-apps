# Idea 09: Neo-Soul & Micro-Swing

- **Theme Slug**: `neo-soul-micro-swing`
- **Category**: Modern Beat Culture / Neo-Soul
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Exploring "Dilla time" and micro-swing polyrhythms by pairing quintuplet (5) and septuplet (7) subdivisions against standard binary (4) quarter notes over lush neo-soul Major 9th and Minor 11th chord progressions.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: F Major 9 (`F3, A4, C5, E5, G5`), C Major 9 (`C3, E4, G4, B4, D5`), A Minor 11 (`A3, C4, E4, G4, D5`), D Minor 9 (`D3, F4, A4, C5, E5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: Quintuplets `5, 10`, Septuplets `7, 14`, against Binary `4, 8`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Deep pocket bass (`F3, C3`) on signature 4; warm 9th extensions on signature 5 and 7 to give a delayed, un-quantized groove feel.
