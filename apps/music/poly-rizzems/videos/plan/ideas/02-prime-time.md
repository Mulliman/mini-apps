# Idea 02: Prime Time Polyrhythms

- **Theme Slug**: `prime-time`
- **Category**: Mathematical / Uncompromising Pulse
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Exploring polyrhythms built exclusively on prime number signatures ($2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19$). Because prime numbers share no common factors (other than 1), prime polyrhythms collide at unpredictable, hypnotic intervals before finally aligning on bar downbeats.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: E Minor 7 (`E3, G4, B4, D5`), D Minor 9 (`D3, F4, A4, C5, E5`), G Dominant 9 (`G3, B4, D5, F5, A5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: Prime signatures: `3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Low prime signatures anchor modal roots; higher prime signatures (11, 13, 17, 19) create sparkling high-pitch textures.

- **Day 1**: `1-prime-3-vs-7.md` — The 3 against 7 Prime Collision (E Minor 7)
- **Day 2**: `2-prime-5-vs-7.md` — The 5 against 7 Hypnotic Interlock (D Minor 7)
- **Day 3**: `3-prime-7-vs-11.md` — The Uncharted 7 against 11 Pulse (G Dominant 7)
- **Day 4**: `4-prime-11-vs-13.md` — Ultra-Dense 11 against 13 Shimmer (C Major 7)
- **Day 5**: `5-prime-triad-3-5-7.md` — The Prime Trinity: 3, 5, 7 (A Minor 9)

- **Day 7**: `7-prime-apex-3-5-7-11-13-17-19.md` — Prime Number Super-Polyrhythm (All prime lanes)
