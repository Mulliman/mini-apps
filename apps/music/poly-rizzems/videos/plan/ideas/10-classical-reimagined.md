# Idea 10: Classical Masterworks Reimagined

- **Theme Slug**: `classical-reimagined`
- **Category**: Classical / Masterwork Reinterpretations
- **Status**: Candidate

## Core Concept
Reinterpreting famous polyrhythmic passages from classical music history (Chopin's *Fantaisie-Impromptu* 3:4, Debussy's *Arabesque No. 1* 3:2, Stravinsky's *Rite of Spring* 5:4, Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata 3:2) using PolyPals visualization and natural note harmonies.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Major 7 (`C3, E4, G4, B4`), A Minor (`A3, C4, E4`), E Minor 7 (`E3, G4, B4, D5`), G Major 7 (`G3, B4, D5, F5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: `2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 12`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Assigning the original melodic and accompaniment lines of famous pieces to corresponding polyrhythmic speed lanes.

## 7-Day Campaign Arc
- **Day 1**: `1-classical-debussy-3-vs-2.md` — Debussy's Arabesque Hemiola (3 against 2, C Major)
- **Day 2**: `2-classical-chopin-4-vs-3.md` — Chopin's Fantaisie-Impromptu (4 against 3, A Minor)
- **Day 3**: `3-classical-beethoven-3-vs-2.md` — Beethoven's Moonlight Triplets (3 against 2, E Minor)
- **Day 4**: `4-classical-stravinsky-5-vs-4.md` — Stravinsky's Rite of Spring (5 against 4, D Minor)
- **Day 5**: `5-classical-brahms-6-vs-4.md` — Brahms' Hemiola Waltz (6 against 4, G Major)
