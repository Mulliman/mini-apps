## Core Concept
Mapping the natural overtone series ratios directly to rhythmic speed signatures. The ratio between pitch frequencies in the harmonic series ($1:2:3:4:5:6:7:8$) is mapped 1:1 to polyrhythmic speed signatures, demonstrating how rhythm and pitch are two ends of the exact same frequency spectrum.

## Musical Blueprint
- **Chords**: C Fundamental Triad and Extensions (`C3, C4, G4, E5, G5, B5`).
- **Rhythmic Families**: Ratios `2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12`.
- **Voicing Strategy**: Bass fundamental ($f_0$) on signature 2 (`C3`), 2nd harmonic on signature 4 (`C4`), 3rd harmonic on signature 6 (`G4`), 4th harmonic on signature 8 (`C5`), 5th harmonic on signature 10 (`E5`).
