# Week 5 Plan: Famous Math Sequences in Polyrhythm

## Theme Overview
Week 5 sonifies the mathematical universe's most celebrated numerical sequences into polyrhythmic structures. Each day translates a complete famous mathematical sequence directly into active polyrhythmic signatures ($1$ to $16$), exploring how metric doubling, quadratic powers, triangular growth, golden spirals, recursive memory, combinatorial leaps, and prime arithmetic interact acoustically. All 7 compositions use exclusively natural notes (C3–C6), single-lane entry pacing, and structured build-and-unwind arcs tailored to ~57–58s for YouTube Shorts retention.

## Schedule & Content Outline
- **Day 1**: `1-powers-of-2.md` — Powers of 2: The Binary Pulse (`1, 2, 4, 8, 16` in C Major Triad)
- **Day 2**: `2-square-numbers.md` — Square Numbers: Quadratic Symmetry (`1, 4, 9, 16` in D Minor)
- **Day 3**: `3-triangular-numbers.md` — Triangular Numbers: Arithmetic Acceleration (`1, 3, 6, 10, 15` in F Major Triad)
- **Day 4**: `4-fibonacci-sequence.md` — Fibonacci Sequence: The Golden Spiral (`1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13` in A Minor 7)
- **Day 5**: `5-tribonacci-sequence.md` — Tribonacci Sequence: 3-Step Recursive Matrix (`1, 2, 4, 7, 13` in G Dominant 9)
- **Day 6**: `6-catalan-numbers.md` — Catalan Numbers: The Combinatorial Leap (`1, 2, 5, 14` in E Minor)
- **Day 7**: `7-prime-numbers.md` — Prime Numbers: The Ultimate Polyrhythm (`2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13` in C Major 7)
