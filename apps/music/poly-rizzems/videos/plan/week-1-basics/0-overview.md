# Week 1 Plan: PolyRhythm Basics

## Theme Overview
Week 1 introduces the fundamental 2-rhythm polyrhythmic collisions (3 vs 4, 2 vs 3, 4 vs 5, 3 vs 5, 3 vs 7, 4 vs 7, 5 vs 7) using clean, accessible triad voicings with a signature 1 tempo anchor.

- **Day 1**: `1-3-vs-4.md` — 3 against 4 Polyrhythm (C Major)
- **Day 2**: `2-2-vs-3.md` — 2 against 3 Polyrhythm (G Major)
- **Day 3**: `3-4-vs-5.md` — 4 against 5 Polyrhythm (A Minor)
- **Day 4**: `4-3-vs-5.md` — 3 against 5 Polyrhythm (F Major)
- **Day 5**: `5-3-vs-7.md` — 3 against 7 Polyrhythm (E Minor)
- **Day 6**: `6-4-vs-7.md` — 4 against 7 Polyrhythm (D Minor)
